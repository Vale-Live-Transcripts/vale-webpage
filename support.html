Questions

When can I get Vale!?

Vale! is in development and not yet on the App Store. This site will say when it is, for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Will my conversations leave my device?

No. Vale! is designed to turn speech into text on the device itself: no audio and no transcripts are sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no account. The privacy policy will describe the app in full before it is released.

Which languages will it understand?

Spanish first, with German and English as options. More languages may follow.

How do I get the best captions in a room?

Put the device close to the people speaking, ideally in the middle of the table, and keep background noise such as the TV low. A small external microphone often helps more than anything else. An iPad propped up in front of you is easier to read than a phone in your hand.

Does Vale! replace hearing aids?

No. Vale! can help you follow a conversation, but it makes mistakes, and it is no substitute for a hearing test, hearing aids or an audiologist’s advice. Don’t rely on it alone in emergencies or for important medical or legal conversations.
